Privacy and Security

PFML is a private site for the players in our Music League. It is run by a league member, not a company, and it has no ads.

Security check

  • New security problems: we went through the security problems announced in the last 30 days for everything PFML is built on: Supabase, GitHub, the sign-in library, and the browsers you use. One mattered: on October 14, Supabase announced a serious flaw in its file storage that could let a stranger replace stored files. Supabase has fixed it on PFML's project, and we checked every stored league file against our own copy: they all match. None of the others affect PFML.
  • Tested from the outside: we tried to get into the site the way a stranger would, without signing in. Every part of the league's data, every members-only feature and the stored files all said no. Nothing could be read or changed.
  • A locked sign-in library: the code that handles signing in is fixed to one exact version and checked against its fingerprint. If anyone tampered with the copy online, your browser would refuse to run it.
  • Checked our own code: anything members type, like replies and comments, is shown as plain text, so it can't run as code in someone else's browser.
  • Checked every day: each morning, an automatic check looks for newly announced problems in Supabase, the sign-in library and the tools that publish the site, makes sure the Supabase services PFML runs on have every fix, and repeats the test from the outside. The time above is when it last passed. If something needs a person to look at it, the time stays put until they have.

What you can do

  • Keep your phone and browser up to date.
  • Only open invite links that come from the league organizer.
  • Sign out (the menu under your initials) on a shared computer.

When you sign in with Google

Signing in shares three things from your Google account: your name, your email address, and Google's ID for your account. PFML uses them only to recognise you and to link you to your player in the league, which happens once, through a personal invite link.

PFML doesn't ask for, and can't see, anything else in your Google account: no Gmail, contacts, Drive, calendar or YouTube. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, the service that stores PFML's accounts and data.

What you add on the site

Votes, reactions and replies you leave on comments are stored with your account and shown to other members, with your player name.

A track you submit on the Submit page is stored without your name or the time you sent it. Which track is yours is kept apart, where nothing on the site can read it, the league organizer included; only you see it, on your own Submit page. Once the round closes, every member can see the list of tracks, and the league organizer turns it into a Spotify playlist and a YouTube playlist on their own accounts. They aren't listed publicly, members open them from the Submit page, and they hold the songs only.

Notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on, from Notifications in the menu under your initials, on each phone or browser you want them on. When you do, PFML keeps, with your account, the address your phone's push service gives it for that device (Apple's, Google's or Mozilla's, depending on the phone and browser). It uses that address only to tell you that a new round has opened or a round's playlist is ready, and the push service carries the message to your phone. PFML keeps a record of which reminders it has sent you, so none comes twice. Turning notifications off on a device deletes its address, and removing your account deletes all of it. PFML doesn't send email.

League data

Standings, submitted tracks, votes and comments come from the league's own Music League exports. They're shown to signed-in members of the league.

What PFML doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone outside the league.
  • No Spotify, YouTube or SoundCloud account is involved. Links to songs are ordinary links to those sites.

Services the site loads from

Like most websites, PFML loads some pieces from other services, which see a normal web request from your browser (including your IP address): GitHub Pages hosts the site, Google Fonts serves the fonts, jsDelivr serves the sign-in library, Supabase handles sign-in and data, and Spotify's image servers serve the album art on round pages. On the Submit page and the repeat checker, a search goes through PFML's Supabase to Spotify or YouTube, and the results' artwork comes from their image servers. The site remembers YouTube searches for a week to save its daily allowance, but never who searched. When you paste a link, your browser asks that service for the track's title and channel, to check it against past rounds. A track plays in Spotify's, YouTube's or SoundCloud's own player, which loads from that service when you paste a link or open a track (YouTube's is its privacy-enhanced player). Pressing Play the round loads each service's player code from that service, and the player remembers in your browser whether you chose YouTube or Spotify copies.

Stored in your browser

Your browser keeps your sign-in session so you stay signed in. While you sign in, it also holds the address of the page you were on, so you land back there, and, if you came from an invite link, that link's code until your account is linked. Signing out clears the session. It also remembers a few choices, on that phone or computer only: dark mode if you turn it on, the newest feature you've seen (so Home can say New), whether you've tapped Get the app, and whether the round player plays YouTube or Spotify copies. In the installed app, it notes that the opening screen has been shown, until you close the app. If you turn on notifications, the browser keeps a small background script from PFML that shows them; it stores nothing and doesn't touch the pages you visit.

Removing your account

Ask the league organizer and your account will be deleted, along with the votes, reactions and replies you added. Tracks you submitted stay in their round's list, with nothing left linking them to you. Your Music League results stay in the league's history, since they come from Music League's own export.
